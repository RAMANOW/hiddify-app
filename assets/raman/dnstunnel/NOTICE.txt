Raman VPN — "DNS قطعی" (shutdown DNS tunnel) tab — third-party notices

This tab runs two open-source DNS-tunnel clients on the phone, each as a local SOCKS5 server on
127.0.0.1 that the Hiddify core (Raman VPN's normal VPN) uses as its only outbound.

   lists come from KevinNet DNS (MIT, THIRD_PARTY-soar-data.md).
